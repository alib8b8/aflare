## When You Need a Commercial License

You need a commercial license if you want to **any** of the following while **not** complying with AGPL:

- Embed aflare in a proprietary product or internal platform without releasing your source code
- Offer aflare-based services to customers while keeping your workflow definitions, integrations, or surrounding code closed
- Remove or replace license/copyright notices in redistributions

You do **not** need a commercial license if:

- You run unmodified aflare internally and can comply with AGPL (including §13 network-use disclosure), or
- Your entire combined work is already AGPL v3 compatible

When in doubt, consult your legal counsel — or just ask us.

## Licensing FAQ

**Is the community edition a trial?**
No. AGPL v3 is a full-featured, permanent license. The commercial license exists for organizations that cannot comply with AGPL's copyleft terms.

**Does buying a commercial license change the community edition?**
No. The community edition remains pure AGPL v3 — a free-software license whose terms we cannot and do not restrict further (AGPL §7).

**Can I fork under AGPL?**
Yes, subject to AGPL terms. Note that "aflare" is a project name; forks should use their own branding.

**What license do contributions use?**
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) — contributors license their work under AGPL v3 and grant the project a parallel right to offer it commercially, which keeps dual licensing viable for everyone.
