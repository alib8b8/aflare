# aflare Corporate Contributor License Agreement / 企业贡献者许可协议

> **Signing / 签署方式**: An authorized representative of the corporation
> replies to a pull request with: "I agree to the terms of
> CLA-CORPORATE.md on behalf of <company name>", or sends a signed copy to
> local_first_agent@126.com.
> 由企业授权代表在 PR 中回复"I agree to the terms of CLA-CORPORATE.md on
> behalf of <公司名>"，或将签署件发送至 local_first_agent@126.com。

For contributions made by employees or contractors in the course of their
work for a corporation. Corporate contributions need this agreement because
employment agreements can complicate who may grant the licenses below —
this document settles it.
本协议适用于企业员工或承包商在职务工作中做出的贡献：劳动关系会使「谁有权授予
下列许可」变得复杂，本文件一次性厘清。
